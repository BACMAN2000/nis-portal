Grade 9 · English · Unit 5

The Double-Edged Sword: Technology, Society and the Environment

Big question — What are the pros and cons of technology at both personal and social levels, and what environmental damage is caused by AI?

5 weeks · You are a critical researcher and advocate. You will analyse articles, debates and reports, compare arguments, and produce your own written and spoken contribution.

🎯 What you will produce

This is the point of the unit — everything else feeds it.
📝

An analytical report

Pros and cons of technology, including its environmental cost. A clear thesis, arguments backed by evidence, and at least one counterargument.

300–350 words · formal register
🎤

An oral presentation

Present your findings on AI and technology's impact, defend your position and answer questions from the floor.

Speak from notes, not from a script
Who is this for? Not only your teacher. You are writing and speaking as an informed citizen taking a position in a real debate that is happening right now — the same debate governments, companies and scientists are having about AI, energy and e-waste.
