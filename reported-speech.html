The essentials
  • The verb usually shifts back one tense: present → past, past → past perfect, will → would, can → could.
  • Pronouns change (I → he/she, my → his/her, you → me…).
  • Time and place words change: now → then, today → that day, tomorrow → the next day, here → there, this → that, last week → the week before.
  • Questions: statement word order (no do/does/did); yes/no → if/whether.
  • Orders/requests: tell + object + (not) to + infinitive → He told me to wait / not to be late.
